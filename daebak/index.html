Daebak — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-02 · 한국어

"Daebak" (the "app") works without any account or sign-up. Your diary and all content you write are stored on your device. The developer does not operate any server that collects or stores personal data. However, when you use the AI features, part or all of your diary and chats is sent to Google's AI as listed below.

1. Data we collect

The developer collects and stores no personal data. However, when you use the features below, the listed data is sent to external services for processing.

What Privacy Mode covers — The "Send only a summary to chat, not the diary text" setting (Privacy Mode) applies only to how today's diary is sent in AI chat. When it is on, instead of today's diary the app sends its first sentence as written, up to about 180 characters (this is not an AI-generated summary). It does not apply to AI feedback, the automatic processing after feedback, hints, quick questions, chat summaries, or the past-diary excerpts and AI Memory sent with chats — using those features sends the data in the table above as is.

2. Data stored on your device

Diary entries, feedback results, expression notes, chat history, AI memory and settings are stored in the app's local storage and are never sent to a developer server. However, parts of your chat history, AI memory and past diaries are sent to Google's AI to generate and summarize chats, as listed above. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Use in-app "Export data" for backups. Android auto-backup is disabled.

3. Your own API key (BYOK)

If you register your own Gemini API key in Settings, it is kept in the device's secure storage and used only to call Google's API.

4. International transfer

The recipients above (Google, Pexels, Unsplash) may process data outside your country. Their own privacy policies apply.

5. Children

This app is not directed to children.

6. Deleting your data

All data lives on your device — deleting it in the app or uninstalling the app removes it immediately. AI chat memory can be deleted individually or entirely in Settings > AI Memory.
