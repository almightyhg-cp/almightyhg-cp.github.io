Daebak — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-09 · 한국어

"Daebak" (the "app") works without any account or sign-up. Your diary and all content you write are stored on your device. The developer does not operate any server that collects or stores personal data. However, when you use the AI features, part or all of your diary and chats is sent to Google's AI as listed below, and usage statistics that contain no diary content are sent to Google Analytics.

1. Data we collect

The developer does not collect or store anything you write, such as your diary or chats. However, when you use the features below, the listed data is sent to external services for processing, and the developer views the usage statistics (Google Analytics) through reports provided by Google.

What Privacy Mode covers — The "Send only a summary to chat, not the diary text" setting (Privacy Mode) applies only to how today's diary is sent in AI chat. When it is on, instead of today's diary the app sends its first sentence as written, up to about 180 characters (this is not an AI-generated summary). It does not apply to AI feedback, the automatic processing after feedback, hints, quick questions, chat summaries, or the past-diary excerpts and AI Memory sent with chats — using those features sends the data in the table above as is.

How Google processes AI requests — Content sent through the AI features is subject to Google's Gemini API terms. Google may log requests and responses for a limited period to detect and prevent violations, and requests processed on the unpaid tier may be used by Google to improve its products and may be read by human reviewers. If you use your own API key (BYOK) in Settings, the tier of that key applies. We recommend not writing sensitive information, such as government ID numbers or health details, in your diary.

2. Data stored on your device

Diary entries, feedback results, expression notes, chat history, AI memory and settings are stored in the app's local storage and are never sent to a developer server. However, parts of your chat history, AI memory and past diaries are sent to Google's AI to generate and summarize chats, as listed above. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Use in-app "Export data" for backups. Android auto-backup is disabled.

3. Your own API key (BYOK)

If you register your own Gemini API key in Settings, it is kept in the device's secure storage and used only to call Google's API.

4. International transfer

The recipients above (Google, Pexels, Unsplash) may process data outside your country. Their own privacy policies apply.

Usage statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase, operated by Google LLC (USA). They are sent over an encrypted connection (TLS) from time to time while you use the app and processed in Google data centers in the United States and elsewhere. The items and purpose are those in the "Usage statistics" row of section 1, and the data is deleted after Google Analytics' data-retention period (2 months).

5. Children

This app is not directed to children.

6. Deleting your data

Data stored on your device is removed immediately when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. AI chat memory can be deleted individually or entirely in Settings > AI Memory. Usage statistics are linked only to the app-instance ID, so the developer cannot tell who you are, and they are deleted automatically after the retention period. Reinstalling the app starts a new ID.
