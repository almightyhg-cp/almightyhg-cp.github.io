Privacy Policy

All-in-One PDF Editor (만능 PDF 편집기) · package com.almightyhg.pdfeditor
Publisher: almightyhg · Effective date: October 20, 2026

In short
  • Your documents and images are opened, edited and converted on your device only. The app does not upload them to any server.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in and no analytics SDK, and the developer does not collect personal data.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and related data as described below.
  • The only other network request the app makes is downloading an optional font file when you ask for it.

1. How the app handles your files

  • When you open a PDF or image (from the file picker, “Open with” or “Share”), the app copies it into its private storage on your device to work on it. All rendering, text placement, signatures, stamps, page operations and conversions run locally on the device.
  • Results are always saved as new files in your phone’s public Download/my_pdf folder (converted images and split files go into subfolders such as Download/my_pdf/<document name>/). Your original file is never modified.
  • Files leave your device only when you choose to share them or open them in another app using Android’s share/open menu. What happens next is governed by the app you pick.

2. Data the app stores on your device

The following data is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

  • Working copies of the document you are editing (temporary; cleaned up when no longer needed).
  • Unsaved-edit backup: while you have unsaved changes, the app records your edits (text you typed, positions of signatures/stamps/drawings, current page) so they can be restored if the app is closed unexpectedly. It is deleted when you save or close the document.
  • Signatures and stamps you create or import, saved as image files so you can reuse them.
  • Downloaded fonts you choose to install.
  • Recent outputs list (file names and their locations, up to 50) and settings such as image format/quality and app language.

Android cloud backup is disabled for this app, so this data is not copied to your Google account backup.

3. Data collected by the developer

None. The developer does not operate any server for this app and does not receive your documents, signatures, stamps, typed text, contacts, location or any other personal information. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob and consent (UMP)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID (and app set ID)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location
  • Device and app information (e.g. device model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions and app usage related to ads (e.g. ad impressions and taps), and diagnostic information about ad performance

This data is collected by Google, not by the developer, and is not linked to your documents. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and can choose whether to allow personalized ads; the choice can be changed later from the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device’s Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Font downloads

Korean/English fonts are built into the app. If you choose to download an extra font (accented Latin – Noto Sans, Japanese – BIZ UDPGothic, Traditional Chinese – Chocolate Classical Sans), the app downloads the font file from raw.githubusercontent.com (the public Google Fonts repository hosted on GitHub). This request contains no document content or personal data from the app; like any web request, the server receives your IP address and standard connection information. GitHub’s privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The “View source” button opens the font’s page on fonts.google.com in your web browser. You can also install a font from a TTF file on your device without any download.

7. Children

The app is a general-purpose document tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling the app (or Settings › Apps › All-in-One PDF Editor › Storage › Clear storage) deletes all data in the app’s private storage: working copies, the unsaved-edit backup, signatures, stamps, fonts, the recent outputs list and settings.
  • Files you saved to Download/my_pdf are your files and remain after uninstalling. Delete them with your file manager if you no longer need them. “Clear list” in the app only removes the list entries, not the files.
  • Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google’s policies; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

9. Security

Font downloads use HTTPS and each file is verified with a SHA-256 checksum before installation. Your documents are not transmitted by the app.
