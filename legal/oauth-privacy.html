Privacy Policy — SmartDaisso Office Tools

Effective 11 September 2026 · Applies to the OAuth application "SmartDaisso Office" (Google Cloud project admin-app-500900)
This is an internal tool. It is operated by SmartDaisso (스마트다있소) and used only by the studio's own staff to read its own advertising and store reports. It is not offered to the public and collects nothing from end users of our apps. Privacy policies for the apps we publish on Google Play are listed on the home page.

1. What the application does

The application signs in with a Google account owned by SmartDaisso and reads reporting data through Google APIs — Google AdMob (earnings and impressions) and, where enabled, Google Play Developer reports. The data is used to produce internal daily briefings about our own apps.

2. Data accessed

  • AdMob account reports: revenue, impressions, clicks and eCPM per app and per ad unit (read-only scope admob.readonly).
  • Google Play Console reports for our own apps: installs, ratings, crashes and store performance (read-only).
  • No personal data of end users is contained in these reports; they are aggregated statistics.

3. Storage and retention

Reports are stored as files on a SmartDaisso-controlled computer and in a private repository accessible only to the studio. The OAuth refresh token is stored locally on that computer with restricted file permissions and is never shared or transmitted elsewhere. Reports are kept for internal bookkeeping and may be deleted at any time.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access page. Revoking access stops all further data collection immediately.

7. Contact

SmartDaisso (스마트다있소) · smartdaitso@gmail.com
