NEON BOWL Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-15 · 한국어

NEON BOWL ("the app", package com.almightyhg.neonbowl) works without an account or sign-up. Records and settings for solo games, the World Tour and practice are stored only on your device. The only feature that sends information to a server is Play with Friends (online friend match), which you choose to use.

1. Information processed when you use Play with Friends

ItemDetailsPurpose
Anonymous user IDA random ID created by Firebase anonymous sign-in (not linked to your name, email or phone number)Tell room members apart, prevent tampered scores
NicknameA name you type (up to 10 characters)Shown to members of the same room

Your nickname and game data are visible only to members of the same room, who need the 6-character room code to join. We do not sell this information or provide it to third parties for advertising or analytics.

2. Service providers and international transfer

Play with Friends runs on Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore) by Google LLC. Game data is stored in the Cloud Firestore Seoul region (Republic of Korea). Google may process standard technical information such as your IP address outside Korea while providing authentication and the service. All transfers are encrypted (HTTPS). See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. Retention and deletion

Room data is stored to run the match and may currently remain on the server after the match ends. To have it deleted, email us your nickname and the room code (if you know it); we will delete that room's data without delay. Records on your device are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings > Apps > NEON BOWL > Storage.

4. Ads, payments and analytics

The app has no ads, no paid purchases or subscriptions, and no usage analytics. It does not use the advertising ID.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. We recommend not using your real name or other identifying information as a nickname.
