Privacy Policy — SafeScan

English · 한국어

Effective: 18 September 2026 (first effective 15 September 2026)

App name: SafeScan (Korean: 세이프스캔) · Package: com.almightyhg.safescan

1. Summary

SafeScan checks a QR code or link for phishing and malicious-app risk before you open it. There is no account, no sign-in and no field for your name or phone number. Your check history stays on your device only and is deleted automatically after 90 days. Codes you save yourself (Favorites and QR Books) stay until you delete them — the app must not quietly erase what you collected on purpose.

Checking a link does, however, mean that the address you are checking sometimes has to leave the device (to resolve a short link, to ask Google's unsafe-site list, or when you tap Report yourself). Section 3 lists exactly what goes where. Nothing else leaves the device.

4. Advertising

This app shows Google AdMob ads.

  • Ads appear only as a banner at the bottom of the screen and as an interstitial on some screen transitions (with per-session and daily caps).
  • No ads are shown during the first 72 hours after installation.
  • No ad is ever placed in front of a safety action on the verdict screen (Close, Report, the 3-second confirmation). There is no limit on the number of checks, and you never have to watch an ad to run a check.
  • App open ads and exit ads are not used.
  • AdMob may process some device information, such as the advertising ID, in order to serve ads. See the Google partner sites policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

6. What is never collected

  • Name, phone number, email, address, date of birth — there is no input field for any of these.
  • Location, contacts, call or SMS logs, list of installed apps.
  • No analytics SDK (such as Firebase Analytics) and no crash-reporting SDK is bundled.
  • Camera frames or original pictures. When a contact, Wi-Fi or payment QR is read, the app does not save, connect or pay on your behalf — it only shows you what the code contains.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Check history: deleted automatically after 90 days on the device. Delete immediately via Library → History → Delete all.
  • Saved codes (Favorites and QR Books): no expiry. You collected them on purpose, so the app never deletes them for you. Remove them at any time under Library → QR Books — one item, a whole book, or [Empty this list] — and they go with the app when you uninstall it. This data is never sent to a server either.
  • Report data: kept in the developer's project for as long as needed for review and blocklist maintenance. Reports carry no name or contact details, so the developer cannot identify an individual user. To request deletion, email the reported address and the approximate time to the address below.
  • Anonymous ID: replaced with a new value when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

8. Third parties and international transfer

  • All transfers listed in section 3 go to services operated by Google LLC (Firebase · Web Risk · AdMob). Those servers may be located outside your country (for example in the United States).
  • The developer does not sell collected information and does not share it with third parties beyond the services above.
  • The blocklist includes, as a supplementary source, the phishing-site URL dataset published by the Korea Internet & Security Agency (KISA) on the Korean Open Government Data Portal (KOGL Type 1). This dataset ships inside the app and contains no user data.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. About the verdicts

Check results are for reference only and do not guarantee 100% safety. SafeScan is not an antivirus and does not remove malicious apps. Before entering personal or financial information, double-check in the official app.
