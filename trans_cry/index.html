Privacy Policy — WhyCry

English · 한국어

Effective date: October 26, 2026

WhyCry (Korean name 응애응애, "the app") is a parenting helper that analyzes a baby's cry on the device to suggest possible reasons for reference, and lets you keep care logs such as feeding, sleep and diapers. This privacy policy explains what information is processed when you use the app.

The developer does not operate any server, and the app does not require sign-up or login. Cry analysis and all records are processed and stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.

1. Information we collect

The developer does not collect or receive your personal information. The app has no backend and no login, and it does not send what you enter to the developer or any third party (the advertising data in Section 3 is the exception and is handled by Google).

The data you enter is stored only inside your device:

  • Baby profile: name (a nickname is fine) and date of birth
  • Care logs: the type and time of breastfeeding, formula (amount), solids, pumping, diapers, sleep, baths and medication, plus any notes you write
  • App settings (language) and small internal values the app keeps for itself (such as when it last checked for updates or asked for a review)

Cry analysis results are shown on screen and are not saved.

You can delete care logs one by one on the records screen, and delete the baby profile with "Reset profile" on the My tab (care logs are kept in that case). Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything. The app does not use Android Auto Backup, so the operating system does not copy your data to the cloud on its own.

2. Microphone and cry analysis

  • The microphone turns on only when you start an analysis, listens for about 4.5 seconds and then turns off. The app does not listen in the background and is not a live baby monitor.
  • The captured sound is analyzed in device memory by an AI model built into the app (YAMNet, TensorFlow Lite) and then discarded. No recording file is created or saved, and the sound is never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • To present the result more helpfully, the app looks at your recent care logs (for example, the time of the last feeding). This calculation also happens entirely on the device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows Google AdMob ads — a banner at the bottom of the screen, an interstitial ad shown after a set number of cry analyses, and an ad inside the confirmation dialog when you exit the app. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and process the following information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (app launches, ad views and taps, etc.)
  • Diagnostics (app launch time, crash and hang rates, energy usage and other ad SDK performance information)

This data is collected by Google, not by the developer, and is encrypted in transit (TLS). Please see Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the app asks for consent under the GDPR through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before showing personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice later from "Ad privacy settings" on the My tab.

Users in the United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for personalized advertising. If a consent/opt-out form is shown, your choice is applied to ad personalization.

4. In-app purchases

The app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Cry analysis, care logs and parenting tips are all free. The app does not request, send or store any payment information.

5. Google Play features (reviews and updates)

The app uses Google Play's in-app review (the rating prompt) and in-app update (new version notice). These are handled by Google Play services, and the app does not send your records with them.

6. Device ID display (for support and QA)

The "Device ID (support & QA)" card at the bottom of the My tab shows this device's Android ID on screen and copies it to the clipboard when tapped. The app never sends this value anywhere; it reaches the developer only if you paste it into a support email yourself. The app also compares this value, on the device, with the developer's list of test devices so that ads are turned off on those test devices only.

7. Permissions and why they are used

  • Microphone: used only when you start a cry analysis (Section 2). If you deny it, care logs and parenting tips still work.
  • Internet: needed only for ads (Google AdMob) and Google Play update checks. Cry analysis and saving records work without a connection.

These two are the permissions the app declares itself. The Google ads SDK and Android libraries also add some permissions automatically (advertising ID, network state, keeping the device awake, running background work, etc.) for the ad serving described in Section 3 and for the libraries' internal operation.

The app does not request camera, location, contacts, phone, or photo and file access permissions.

8. Children

The app is made for parents and caregivers and is not designed to be used directly by children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Information about the baby (name, date of birth and care logs) is entered by the caregiver, stored only on the caregiver's device, and never sent to the developer.

9. Data retention and security

Because the developer has no server, the developer does not retain your data. All app data is stored on your device and is removed when you delete records, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Information processed by Google for advertising is handled under Google's policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in the US), you may have rights to access, delete or restrict the processing of personal information and to opt out of personalized ads. Since the developer holds no personal information, such requests mostly concern Google's advertising data — please see Google's policies above. You can also contact us at the address below.

11. Not a medical device

WhyCry's analysis is an estimate for reference and not a medical diagnosis. WhyCry is not a medical device, and no result replaces the judgment of a doctor or professional. If you are worried about your baby's condition, contact a medical provider or professional right away.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may change. If there is a significant change, we will update the "Effective date" above and, where needed, let you know through the app or the store listing.
