How we build

Rules the whole studio follows — not decided app by app

01 On the device, no servers

Word recognition, cry analysis and sleep labelling all happen on the phone. Data that never leaves can't leak.

02 Personal data kept to a minimum

Your records stay on the device and are excluded from cloud auto-backup. No account needed — just open the app.

03 Free with ads, no purchases

No in-app purchases. Every feature stays open; only the parts that cost us money are extended by watching an ad.

04 Real devices before release

Every release build is installed on a real phone and checked before it is sent to the store.

Contact

Bug reports, suggestions and collaboration proposals are all welcome by email. We usually reply within a few days.
