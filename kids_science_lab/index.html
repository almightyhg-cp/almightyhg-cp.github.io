Privacy Policy — My Home Lab

English · 한국어

Effective: 10 September 2026

App name: My Home Lab (Korean: 우리집실험실) · Package: com.almightyhg.kids_science_lab

1. Summary

This app collects no information that identifies you. There is no account and no sign-in. Experiment progress, photos, journal entries and badges are stored on your device only. Neither the developer nor any third party can see them.

2. What the app stores on your device

ItemContentsWhereIf you uninstall
Experiment progressWhich experiment and step you are on, what you chose when observing, measured valuesDevice storage (SQLite)Deleted with the app
Experiment journalResults and findings you write yourself, photos you attachApp-private folder (other apps cannot read it)Deleted with the app
RewardsStars and badgesDevice storageDeleted with the app
SettingsGrade group, sound and notification togglesDevice storageDeleted with the app
  • This data is never sent to a server. The app has no server.
  • It is also excluded from cloud backup (allowBackup=false plus backup exclusion rules).
  • You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Guardian menu → Reset data.

  • Gallery read permissions (READ_MEDIA_*) are not requested.

4. Advertising

This app shows Google AdMob ads.

  • Ads appear only as a bottom banner on the Home, Experiments and Journal screens and as one interstitial per session on the experiment completion screen.
  • There are no ads on the player screen where a child runs an experiment, and none in the guardian menu.
  • No ads are shown during the first 72 hours after installation.
  • App open ads, exit ads and rewarded ads are not used.
  • In line with the Google Play Families policy, ad requests are tagged for child treatment with a content rating of G, and personalised advertising is not used.
  • AdMob may process some device information, such as the advertising ID, in order to serve ads. See the Google partner sites policy for details.
  • Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom see an ad consent dialog (a Google-certified CMP) on first launch, and can change that choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

5. What is never collected

  • Name, age, date of birth, school, address, phone number, email — there is no input field for any of these.
  • Location, contacts, call logs, list of installed apps.
  • No analytics SDK (such as Firebase Analytics) is bundled.
  • A shared journal card contains only the photo, the experiment name, the sentence you wrote and the app name. There is no field for a name or a school, so a child's personal information has no route onto the card.

6. Third parties and international transfer

  • The developer collects no user data, so there is no data to share with third parties.
  • Device information processed by Google AdMob while serving ads is governed by Google's policies.

7. Protection of children

  • The app is designed for a guardian and a primary-school child to use together (mixed audience).
  • Risky steps require a guardian to press and hold for three seconds before the app moves on.
  • Experiments that use alcohol can only be started while unlocked from the guardian menu.
  • No personal information is collected from children.
