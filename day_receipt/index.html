Privacy Policy — Day Receipt

English · 한국어

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Day Receipt ("the App", "we", "us") lets you log your mood, a choice you agonized over, and a purchase you regret as a single daily "receipt," for self-reflection. This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use the App.

We do not operate our own servers and the App does not require an account or login. All entries are stored entirely on your device, and your entries are never transmitted to us.

1. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect or receive any personal information from you. The App has no backend and no sign-in, and does not transmit your entries to us or to any third party (except the ad data described in Section 3, which is processed by Google).

Data you enter is stored locally on your device only:

  • Mood, energy level, a one-line note, and "small win" entries
  • Choices you log (A/B-style decisions) and satisfaction ratings
  • Spending-regret entries: amount (optional), reason, and a regret score — no bank, card, or account linkage of any kind
  • A 4-digit app-lock PIN, if you set one (stored on-device only)
  • App preferences (theme, reminder time, currency symbol, language)

You can delete entries in the App at any time, and erase everything by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. The App does not use Android Auto Backup — your data is not copied to any cloud backup by the operating system.

2. Export and import (your own file)

You may export all of your entries to a JSON file and import it back later (for example, when switching phones). This file is created and read only at your request; the App does not upload it anywhere on your behalf. What you do with the exported file (e.g., where you save or share it) is under your control.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob: a banner while you browse, an occasional interstitial after you share a receipt, and an optional rewarded ad that unlocks additional receipt themes for the day. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and process information to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), device identifiers
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, coarse location derived from IP)
  • IP address and ad interaction data

This data is collected by Google, not by us. Please review Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before serving personalized ads, in line with GDPR. You can change or withdraw your choice later from the App's settings (Ad privacy).

Users in the United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Where a consent/opt-out form is presented, your choice is applied to ad personalization.

4. In-app purchases

The App has no in-app purchases and no subscription. Every feature — mood, choice and spending-regret logging, reports, calendar, weekly/monthly summary receipts, PIN lock, JSON export/import, and daily reminders — is free with no usage limit. The optional extra receipt themes are unlocked for the day by watching a rewarded ad, not by payment. The App therefore never requests, transmits, or stores payment details.

5. Sharing feature

You can share a finished receipt image via the Android share sheet. The image is generated on your device and handed to the app you choose; nothing is rendered to an image, uploaded, or kept by us unless you ask for it.

6. Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: to send your daily reminder at the time you choose. If you turn reminders off, this permission is not needed.
  • Receive boot completed: to re-schedule your reminder after you restart your phone.
  • Internet: required only to load and display ads (Google AdMob). Saving entries and computing reports never requires a network connection.

The three permissions above are the ones the App declares itself. The Google advertising SDK and the notification library also add permissions of their own (advertising ID, network state, vibration, wake lock and similar); these serve the advertising described in section 3 and the reminder behaviour described above.

The App does not request camera, location, contacts, or microphone permissions.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and security

Because we have no servers, we do not store your data. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you delete entries, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. An optional 4-digit PIN can restrict local access to the App. Information processed by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA/CPRA in the US), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and to opt out of personalized ads. Because the developer holds no personal data, such requests primarily concern Google's ad data — see Google's policies above. You may also contact us using the details below.

10. Not a financial, medical or counseling service

Day Receipt is a self-reflection tool. It is not a budgeting, banking, or asset-management app, and it is not counseling or a medical service. It does not replace professional financial, psychological, or medical advice.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, within the App or store listing.
