REAL ARCHERY Privacy Policy

한국어

Effective date: November 25, 2026

SmartDaitso publishes REAL ARCHERY for Android. This policy explains what happens to information when you play, use online matches, or see advertising. Contact us at smartdaitso@gmail.com with questions or requests. We do not sell your personal data.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress, records and settings on your device using local files and Android's local preferences. These game saves are not uploaded to us. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove them. We do not provide a game account or in-app purchases.

Optional tilt aiming uses your device's gyroscope or attitude sensors. Sensor readings stay on the device and are used to control aiming. The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, device location or storage. It uses internet and network-state access for online play and advertising, and the Android advertising ID permission for AdMob.

Playing online with a friend

Online play is optional and needs an internet connection. When you use it, the game signs in to Google Firebase anonymously. Firebase supplies an anonymous user ID and authentication credentials so the service can recognize the same installation and control access to a match. You do not create a named account or provide an email address to play.

The game sends your chosen nickname and match room information to Firebase Realtime Database. Room data includes the invitation code, match settings, players' anonymous IDs, each arrow's score and target position, and connection-presence heartbeats. Your opponent can see your nickname and match information. A nickname is free text: please do not enter your real name, contact details or other personal information. There is no text chat.

We use this information to connect the two players, operate and resume matches, display results and maintain access and connection security. Rooms are intended to be short-lived: the host normally deletes a finished room when leaving, and old rooms can be cleaned up on a later connection. Cleanup is not a guaranteed automatic deletion at a fixed time, so abandoned room data may remain until cleanup or a deletion request. The anonymous authentication record can remain until deleted. Local progress and offline records are not part of the room data.

Advertising and Google services

We use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide ads. Google may collect and share the device's IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location; advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, other device or account identifiers; app and ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views; and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention and security. The game does not request location permission or collect precise GPS location. Google may process data under its own terms as well as provide services to us; advertising partners may also receive data through Google's advertising services. See Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising information.

Interstitial ads can appear when leaving a results screen, subject to limits; they do not appear in the first 72 hours. Rewarded ads are offered when you choose to watch one for an eligible retry or an additional online game. If the ad cannot load, the reward is granted. There are no banner ads and no ads while aiming or shooting.

Google provides Firebase hosting and anonymous authentication for online play on our behalf. Google also provides AdMob and consent services. We share the data needed for those services as described above; Google may use advertising data for its own advertising purposes. We do not use Firebase Analytics. Google retains information under its own policies and settings; we do not control its retention periods.

Your choices and deletion

Where required, a consent form asks about advertising choices. When available, you can revisit those choices in Settings > Privacy options. Android settings also let you reset or delete the advertising ID and manage advertising personalization; the exact menu depends on your device. These controls do not necessarily stop all ads. You can choose not to use online play and can clear local game data through Android settings or uninstall the app.

To request deletion of online data or ask about your information, email smartdaitso@gmail.com. Tell us you are contacting us about REAL ARCHERY and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant data without sending sensitive information. Because online identities are anonymous, we may need additional information to locate a record, and may be unable to identify it from a nickname alone. We will handle requests subject to applicable law and may retain information where required by law or for legitimate security needs. Contact Google separately about data it controls.

Children, security and changes

REAL ARCHERY is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Online communications use HTTPS encryption in transit. We use access controls for online rooms, but no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. We may update this policy as the game or legal requirements change; the updated policy and effective date will appear on this page.
