Privacy Policy — Anger Lab

English · 한국어

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Anger Lab ("the App", "we", "us") puts 90 seconds between a flash of anger and your reaction, then lets you note what set it off so you can look back on it yourself. This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use the App.

We do not operate our own servers and the App does not require an account or login. Your anger and mood entries and photos are stored entirely on your device and are never transmitted to us.

1. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect or receive any personal information from you. The App itself contains no code that talks to a server, and it does not transmit your entries to us or to any third party (except the ad data described in Section 3, which is processed by Google).

The following data you enter is stored locally on your device only:

  • Anger entries: intensity, mood beforehand, body state, place, situation, what touched the nerve, how you reacted, the feelings underneath, regret, and an optional note
  • Heat ratings before and after cool-down and breathing sessions, and coach history
  • Anchors: one photo you pick from your gallery and a sentence you choose in advance — the photo is copied into the App's private folder, never uploaded
  • Your preferred region (province/city), used to show local help services
  • App preferences (vibration, sound, language, lock on/off and similar)

You can delete entries in the App at any time, and erase everything by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. The App does not use Android Auto Backup or device-to-device transfer, so the operating system does not copy your entries to a cloud backup or a new phone.

2. Export (your own file)

At your request, the App writes your entries to a JSON or CSV file and hands it to the Android share sheet. The file is created only when you ask, and the App does not upload it anywhere on your behalf. Where you save or share it is under your control — it contains sensitive emotional records, so please store it with care.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob. For your safety, no ads appear during breathing or cool-down sessions, while you write an entry, or on the help (SOS) screens. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and process the following to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), app set ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (app launches, ad taps and views)
  • Diagnostics (app launch time, crash/hang rate, energy usage, and other ad SDK performance data)

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit (TLS). Your anger and mood entries and photos are never passed to the ad SDK. Please review Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before serving personalized ads, in line with GDPR. You can change or withdraw your choice later from the App's settings (Ad privacy).

Users in the United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Where a consent/opt-out form is presented, your choice is applied to ad personalization.

4. In-app purchases

The App has no in-app purchases and no subscription. It never requests, transmits, or stores payment details.

5. Help services

Phone numbers and links on the help screen only open your phone or browser app when you tap them. The App cannot see whether you called or what was said, and records nothing about it.

6. Permissions and why they are used

  • Internet: required only to load and display ads (Google AdMob). Saving and analysing entries never requires a network connection.
  • Biometric / screen lock (if you turn the lock on): to lock your records. The operating system performs the check and the App only receives success or failure; no biometric data reaches or is stored by the App.
  • Photo picker: to choose an anchor photo using the Android photo picker. The App only accesses the single photo you pick.

The Google advertising SDK also adds permissions of its own (advertising ID, network state and similar) for the advertising described in Section 3. The App does not request location, contacts, or microphone permissions.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and security

Because we have no servers, we do not store your data. All app data lives on your device and is removed when you delete entries, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. The optional lock can restrict local access to the App. Information processed by Google for ads is handled under Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA/CPRA in the US, the Personal Information Protection Act in Korea), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, and to opt out of personalized ads. Because the developer holds no personal data, such requests primarily concern Google's ad data — see Google's policies above. You may also contact us using the details below.

10. Not a medical or counseling service

Anger Lab is a self-management tool for noticing your own anger and taking a pause. It is not a medical device or therapy, and it promises no diagnosis or treatment outcome. If you are at risk of harming yourself or others, contact your local emergency number or a professional service right away.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, within the App or store listing.
