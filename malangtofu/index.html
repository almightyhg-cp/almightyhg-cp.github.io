Malang Tofu Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-13 · 한국어

Malang Tofu ("the app", Google Play edition) works without an account or sign-up. Your tofu's state and game records are stored only on your device. The developer does not run any server that collects or stores personal information.

1. Information we collect

The developer collects no personal information. The game works offline; the network is used only for the purposes below.

FeatureData sentRecipientPurpose
AdsAdvertising ID and standard ad SDK dataGoogle AdMobBanner and rewarded ads (after consent in the EEA/UK)
Updates and ratingsStandard Google Play dataGoogle PlayNew-version prompts, rating sheet

2. Data stored only on your device

Your tofu's needs (fullness, water, mood, energy), beans, growth stage, costumes, collection, check-in history and sound/vibration settings are stored only on the device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app. Android auto-backup and device transfer are disabled.

3. Ads and international transfer

Ads are served by Google AdMob; the advertising ID and standard data may be sent to and processed by Google outside your country under the Google ads policy. Users in the EEA/UK are asked for consent first and can change it any time in Settings > Ad privacy. Rewarded ads play only when you tap for them, and rewards are in-game beans only (no cash or points).

4. Payments

The app has no paid purchases or subscriptions.

5. Children

The app is not primarily directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

6. Deleting your data

All data lives on your device, so uninstalling deletes it immediately. To reset only the game records, clear storage in Android Settings > Apps > Malang Tofu > Storage.

Privacy questions: smartdaitso@gmail.com
