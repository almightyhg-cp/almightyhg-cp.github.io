Privacy Policy — Ppajak

English · 한국어

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Ppajak (Korean name 빠작, "the app") is a tactile ASMR app for enjoying the sound, vibration and cracks of breaking wax balls. This privacy policy explains what information is processed when you use the app.

The developer does not operate any server, and the app does not require sign-up or login. Breaking balls and all records are processed and stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.

1. Information we collect

The developer does not collect or receive your personal information. The app has no backend and no login, and it does not send its records to the developer or any third party (the advertising data in Section 2 is the exception and is handled by Google).

The data the app uses is stored only inside your device:

  • How many balls you broke on each day (the records on the stats screen)
  • Progress such as the balls you have collected, unlock status, free balls left today and freezing status
  • App settings (haptic strength, the volume of the three sound layers, left-hand mode, film-mode background, language) and small internal values the app keeps for itself (such as ad spacing, when it last checked for updates or asked for a review)

You can erase all of this by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. The app does not use Android Auto Backup, so the operating system does not copy your data to the cloud on its own.

The app does not use the microphone or the camera. Film mode simply hides the buttons to leave a clean screen; the app does not record or save the screen.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows Google AdMob ads — a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad when you open the collection (at most three a day), and rewarded ads that you choose to watch (refilling balls, instant freezing and ball draws). No banner or full-screen ads are shown for the first 72 hours after installation, and full-screen ads never appear while you are breaking a ball. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and process the following information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (app launches, ad views and taps, etc.)
  • Diagnostics (app launch time, crash and hang rates, energy usage and other ad SDK performance information)

This data is collected by Google, not by the developer, and is encrypted in transit (TLS). Please see Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the app asks for consent under the GDPR through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before showing personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice later from "Ad privacy settings" in Settings.

Users in the United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for personalized advertising. If a consent/opt-out form is shown, your choice is applied to ad personalization.

3. In-app purchases

The app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Breaking, kneading the filling, the freezer, film mode and your records are all free, and on days you run out of free balls you can refill them by watching a rewarded ad. The app does not request, send or store any payment information.

4. Google Play features (reviews and updates)

The app uses Google Play's in-app review (the rating prompt) and in-app update (new version notice). These are handled by Google Play services, and the app does not send your records with them.

5. Device ID display (for support and QA)

The "Device ID (support & QA)" item at the bottom of Settings shows this device's Android ID on screen and copies it to the clipboard when tapped. The app never sends this value anywhere; it reaches the developer only if you paste it into a support email yourself. The app also compares this value, on the device, with the developer's list of test devices so that ads are turned off on those test devices only.

6. Permissions and why they are used

  • Internet: needed only for ads (Google AdMob) and Google Play update checks. Breaking balls, sound, vibration and saving records work without a connection.

This is the permission the app declares itself. The vibration (haptics) library also adds the vibrate permission, and the Google ads SDK and Android libraries add some permissions automatically (advertising ID, network state, keeping the device awake, running background work, etc.) for the haptic effects, the ad serving described in Section 2 and the libraries' internal operation.

The app does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, phone, or photo and file access permissions.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and security

Because the developer has no server, the developer does not retain your data. All app data is stored on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Information processed by Google for advertising is handled under Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in the US), you may have rights to access, delete or restrict the processing of personal information and to opt out of personalized ads. Since the developer holds no personal information, such requests mostly concern Google's advertising data — please see Google's policies above. You can also contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change. If there is a significant change, we will update the "Effective date" above and, where needed, let you know through the app or the store listing.
